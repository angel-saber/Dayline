Use made-up notes only. This lab simulates a server and two devices in your browser. It does not connect to Supabase or modify your Dayline account. It has not received an independent security review.

Create a test vault, or unlock an existing lab vault.

1. Unlock your test vault

Use a separate passphrase, different from any real account password. Sign-in and encryption unlocking are separate.

Your private recovery key

This key unlocks your test notes. Keep it somewhere private, separate from the encrypted backup. It is not stored by this lab.

2. Write, sync, and lock

Save and sync on Desktop. Switch to Phone, enter the vault passphrase, and unlock. Each device keeps its own encrypted draft.

Change the vault passphrase

Enter the current passphrase or recovery key above. This changes vault unlocking, not an account password. Old downloaded backups still accept their original credentials.

3. Check what is stored

Only wrapped keys and ciphertext should appear in the simulated server. Note titles, dates, categories, and settings are inside the encrypted snapshot.
